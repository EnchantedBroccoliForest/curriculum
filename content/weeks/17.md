## Derivation or exercise, stated in full

Closed-book: backprop through a transformer block; the ELBO; policy gradient with baseline. Competency rows 2–6 set

Derivation 1 (backprop through a transformer block). Setting: one pre-norm decoder block acting on $X \in \mathbb R^{T\times d}$, single attention head for simplicity, with $Q = \mathrm{LN}(X)W_Q$, $K = \mathrm{LN}(X)W_K$, $V = \mathrm{LN}(X)W_V$, $A = \mathrm{softmax}(QK^\top/\sqrt{d} + M)$ with causal mask $M$, $Y = X + AVW_O$, $Z = Y + \mathrm{MLP}(\mathrm{LN}(Y))$ with a two-layer MLP and a pointwise nonlinearity. Given $\partial \mathcal L/\partial Z \in \mathbb R^{T\times d}$, derive $\partial\mathcal L/\partial X$ and the gradients with respect to every weight matrix. State the shape of every intermediate. Treat LayerNorm's Jacobian as a named sub-result and derive it separately. Multi-head is optional; if included, state how the head dimension enters.

Derivation 2 (the ELBO). Setting: observed $x$, latent $z$, generative model $p_\theta(x\mid z)p(z)$, variational family $q_\phi(z\mid x)$. Derive $\log p_\theta(x) \ge \mathbb E_{q_\phi}[\log p_\theta(x\mid z)] - \mathrm{KL}(q_\phi(z\mid x)\,\|\,p(z))$ by two routes (Jensen; and the exact decomposition $\log p_\theta(x) = \mathrm{ELBO} + \mathrm{KL}(q_\phi(z\mid x)\,\|\,p_\theta(z\mid x))$), identify the gap, and state the condition for equality. Then state what is needed to take a gradient of the ELBO with respect to $\phi$ when $q_\phi$ is Gaussian (the reparameterization), without deriving the VAE training loop.

Derivation 3 (policy gradient with baseline). Setting as in week 15: episodic MDP, horizon $T$, differentiable stochastic policy. Derive $\nabla_\theta J(\theta)$ in reward-to-go form, then show that a state-dependent baseline $b(s_t)$ does not change the expectation. State the assumptions used at each step.

Rules: no notes, no code, no model. Time limit two hours for the three. Record the outcome and the date in the derivations ledger with the closed-book flag; set competency rows 2–6 from the result and from the Phase 1 artifacts (toy GPT, ResNet notebook write-up, fifteen paper notes); set each retest date to 4–6 weeks out.
